## New

- Chat: share photos, videos, files and text into a chat from any other app.
- Interface: on a wide phone or a small tablet the lists and the open chat stand side by side.
- Interface: "Report a bug" now sends the report straight from the app.

## Improved

- Interface: refreshed look with tabs, glass panels, message bubbles and headers.
- Chat: connection quality is measured more carefully, so the app keeps a working route longer.

## Fixed

- Interface: an unfolded phone (Galaxy Fold, Oppo Find) now shows two columns.
- Chat: a new device shows the restore of history only once, not with every new month.
- Chat: a file of the history that fails to download waits before the next try.
- Sync: large files now download correctly on Windows.
- Sync: a problem with the disk of one device no longer stops cleaning up old data.
- Interface: buttons and fields no longer hide under the camera cutout of the iPhone.
