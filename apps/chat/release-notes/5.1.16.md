## New

- Notifications: on Linux, names and text are hidden while the screen is locked.

## Improved

- Interface: on Android, the system backup and phone transfer no longer copy the app's data.
- Interface: erasing all data now asks you to confirm it, with your unlock secret when the app lock is on.
- Messages: while the app is locked, chats can no longer be read or written.
- Messages: changes to a group's members and roles now settle the same way on every device.
- Messages: someone removed from a group can no longer slip messages into its past.
- Messages: a blocked contact stays blocked, also in history that loads later.
- Messages: a group invite from someone you do not know waits in the list without a notification.
- Photos and files: pictures on servers you do not know are downloaded only when you tap them.
- Photos and files: a received message can no longer make the app open or send one of your own files.
- Notifications: pictures in notifications come only from avatars already on your device.
- Calls: group calls now follow the setting to connect through relays only.

## Fixed

- Calls: a slow call server no longer holds up your other calls.
- Interface: going back from the help page of Veydan Cloud returns you to where you were.
