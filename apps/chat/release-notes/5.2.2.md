## Improved

- Interface: on Mac the app now opens without a security warning.

## Fixed

- Photos and files: on Android the gallery, the camera and saving a picture ask for permission again after the app was reopened.
- Photos and files: on Android a refused camera permission is now said instead of "the camera did not open".
- Notifications: on Android "Don't ask again" is respected again.
- Calls: on Android a call asks for its permissions again after the app was reopened.
- Contacts: unblocking a contact no longer rolls back on your other devices.
